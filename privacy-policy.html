Privacy Policy

App: Space Cargo (tadpole.spacecargosort) Effective: 2 October 2026

In short

  • Space Cargo has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your phone.
  • Ads (Google AdMob), purchases (Google Play) and usage analytics (Google Firebase) are provided by Google, which processes some device data as described below.
  • In the EEA, UK and US states with privacy laws, you choose whether ads are personalised, and you can change that at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

03 GOOGLE

Services from Google

Google AdMob (ads)

The game shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, and occasional interstitial ads between levels. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalise them. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Firebase Analytics

Firebase tells us, in aggregate, how the game is played: for example, which levels are hard and where players stop. It does not identify you by name. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. We receive a purchase token and order ID so the game can deliver and restore your item. See policies.google.com/privacy.

04 CHOICE

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and US states): the game asks for your ad consent the first time you play. You can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Ad personalisation, anywhere: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Remove Ads: this purchase stops interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available, and only play when you choose to watch one.
  • Delete everything on your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Space Cargo → Storage.
05 KEEP

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Firebase Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Google keeps advertising and purchase data under its own policies, linked above.

06 RIGHTS

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, US state privacy laws, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Because the game has no accounts, most of your data is only on your phone. For analytics data held by Firebase, email us and include the app-instance ID if you can. We will respond within 30 days.

07 AGE

Children

Space Cargo is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help delete it.

08 SAFE

Security and transfers

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit. Google may process it on servers outside your country, including in the United States, with the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

09 CHANGES

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes on Google Play.
